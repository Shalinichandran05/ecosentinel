System Modules

The system separates administrative control, field reporting, and analytical decision support while keeping all records in a centralized data store.

Admin Side

Manage user accounts and system access, verify incident records, monitor overall conflict data, and use dashboards and reports for decision-making.

Officer / User Side

Login securely, report new wildlife conflict incidents, enter location and damage details, and view recorded incidents with risk information.

System / BI Module

Store incident data centrally, analyze conflict patterns, generate visual dashboards, and provide insights for preventive action.

Core Capabilities

EcoSentinel converts incident records into a structured operational knowledge base for planning and review.

  • Centralized storage of historical human-wildlife conflict records.
  • Risk categorization into Low, Medium, and High conflict zones.
  • Interactive visualizations for trends, species analysis, and hotspots.
  • Searchable records that preserve institutional knowledge.
  • Continuous updates as new field incidents are added.
  • Decision support for patrol planning and resource allocation.
